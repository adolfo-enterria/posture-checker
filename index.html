    How it works

    1. Frame yourself. Head and both shoulders in view, facing the screen.
    2. Calibrate. Sit in your best posture for 3 seconds. That becomes your target.
    3. Work normally. If you drift from it for a few seconds, you get a gentle beep and a notification, even when this tab is in the background.

    The pose model runs inside your browser. No video or images are uploaded or saved. Only your calibration and daily totals are stored, in this browser.
